Reverse Garbage Intro

Want to take on this challenge with me? Let's go!!!

This time, you are not an actuary. You are working in a consulting team that provides free support to social enterprises, because you want to devote some effort to encouraging environmentally friendly organisations.

You are so lucky to work with this team because all of you share the same value: trying your best to help. The problem faced by the social enterprise, Reverse Garbage, is that they are wondering why their workshops are not working well for company team-building activities.

You are given full freedom to explore the problem and tell them why, since they also operate as a non-profit organisation and have limited time to focus on the business side.

Your team attended two meetings with the CEO, experienced their workshop in person, designed a complete survey for participating organisations, and finally provided suggestions through a complete marketing research process.

After four weeks of collaboration, our team finally delivered a complete analysis and set of recommendations. RG was very impressed and inspired by our work: Reverse Garbage final report ↗

Airbnb Pricing Model Intro

Want to take on this challenge with me? Why not!!!

This time, you are planning to become an Airbnb investor in Melbourne. But before putting your money into a property, you want to know more than just: “Is this a good location?”

Luckily, you have nearly 9,000 Airbnb listings in front of you, containing information about price, location, room type, host behaviour, reviews, and many other features.

Now it is time to use your data analytics and machine learning skills to tell yourself:

  • What makes an Airbnb listing more likely to achieve a high customer rating?
  • As an investor, what kind of property and hosting strategy should you choose?
  • Most importantly, how much should the property actually be priced at?

After testing different statistical and machine learning models, you finally found your answers: Final report ↗
