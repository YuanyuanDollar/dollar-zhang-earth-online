A “What If?” Easter Egg

My family runs an ink manufacturing business and, at the time, was planning to expand into the Southeast Asian market. I wondered: Would working in the family business actually suit me better? So I decided to get involved and find out for myself.

I started by helping build a new website for its overseas expansion and thinking about how the business could present itself to international customers.

The experience gave me a taste of business, design, and entrepreneurship, but more importantly, it helped me understand myself better. I realised that while I enjoy the business side of things, I’m most engaged when my work also involves analytical thinking and problem-solving.

That experience didn’t pull me away from actuarial science. In a way, it helped me understand why I’m still choosing it.
